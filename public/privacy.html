Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Krazy Data (www.krazydata.site) is a sports research site. This page explains what we collect when you use it, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address and password when you sign up with email. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are never stored by us in readable form. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account.
  • Your profile and settings. The display name and photo you choose, your site settings, your saved picks and slips, and Krazy Bot's memory of your recent questions and favorite stats, so they follow you to every device you sign in on.
  • Alerts. If you turn alerts on, your device's notification address, so we can send you announcements and tell you when a line on one of your saved picks moves or is pulled.
  • Community posts. Posts and reactions you share in the Community feed. Other members see them with your display name and photo.
  • Slip screenshots. When you upload a slip to the Slip Analyzer, the image is sent to a service that reads the picks from it. We don't keep the image.
  • Activity. When you last visited, for our member list, and the basic request logs every website host keeps (such as IP address, browser type and time).
  • Cookies and browser storage. Sign-in cookies keep you signed in. Your browser also saves boards and preferences so pages load quickly. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in on your devices.
  • To run the features you use, and keep your settings and saved picks in sync.
  • To send the alerts you've turned on.
  • To keep the site safe, for example by limiting repeated sign-in attempts.
  • To manage memberships, such as early access or pausing an account that breaks our Terms.

We don't sell your personal information and we don't use it for advertising.

Google sign-in

If you choose Continue with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture. We use them to create and sign in to your Krazy Data account and to show your profile on the site. We don't use Google user data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone except the service providers below that run sign-in for us.

Krazy Data's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Krazy Data's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the site for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase, for accounts, sign-in and storing your profile, settings and saved picks.
  • Vercel, which hosts the site.
  • Google, if you sign in with Google.
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla notification services, to deliver alerts to your device.
  • Groq, which reads the picks from slip screenshots you upload.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the site and its members.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open. You can turn alerts off and change your profile at any time. To delete your account and the information tied to it, email us at CONTACT_EMAIL from the address on your account.

Security

Every connection to the site is encrypted, and your sign-in is kept in cookies that scripts on the page can't read. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Age

Krazy Data is not for anyone under 18, or under the legal age for sports wagering or fantasy contests where they live.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
